# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Primary users are restaurant owners who need an immediate, plain-language answer to whether a location is safe, and engineers who need traceable sensor, network, and analysis detail. Owners act during normal operation and incidents; engineers investigate events, validate data quality, and maintain devices.

## Product Purpose

Autonomous LPG Safety turns multi-variable LPG telemetry into understandable safety states, evidence-backed risk assessment, and an auditable event history. Success means a user can identify the current safety state in seconds and an engineer can explain the data and reasoning behind it without relying on a black-box model.

## Brand Personality

Calm, clear, trustworthy. The interface should reduce cognitive load in normal conditions and become direct, legible, and decisive when an event requires attention.

## Anti-references

Avoid cyberpunk control rooms, decorative "AI" imagery, dark dashboards overloaded with glowing metrics, and interfaces that imply a disconnected sensor is safe. Do not use an LLM as the authority for safety decisions.

## Design Principles

1. Put the current safety state before raw telemetry.
2. Show the evidence behind every risk conclusion.
3. Separate normal, unsafe, and unknown monitoring states.
4. Keep analytical depth available without overwhelming owners.
5. Use one normalized data model from simulation through hardware integration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Never encode status with color alone; pair it with text and icons. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic controls, and reduced-motion preferences. Critical content must remain readable at zoom and on narrow screens.
